Rules at a glance
  • Use the primary logo on light backgrounds by default
  • Use reversed on dark or photographic backgrounds
  • Icon-only for small spaces: favicons, app icons, social
  • Monochrome for one-color print, embossing, engraving
  • Never stretch, rotate, recolor, or apply effects to the mark
  • Never reproduce below minimum size: {{MIN_SIZE}}
